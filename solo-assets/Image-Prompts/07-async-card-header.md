A photorealistic editorial product photograph used as the top-of-card image on the "Async — Resonance OS (Notion)" card of a premium founder-coaching landing page. The image must communicate "this is a thoughtfully-designed Notion workspace running on a real laptop" at a glance, while still feeling editorial rather than promotional.

CRITICAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
- The laptop screen must clearly show a Notion-like interface — a real software workspace with a left sidebar of page names and a main content area, NOT a generic UI mockup, NOT abstract shapes, NOT lorem-ipsum gibberish.
- The Notion-like UI rendered on the screen must be legible enough that the structure reads instantly. Real-looking page names and section labels matter more than perfect Notion-brand accuracy.

COMPOSITION:
- Wide landscape ~16:9. A modern silver or space-grey laptop (MacBook-style, no visible brand logo) sits centred on a warm honey-toned wood desk, viewed from a slight three-quarter angle that lets the viewer see the screen clearly without losing context of the desk surface.
- The screen fills roughly 55–65% of the frame.
- One small adjacent object for warmth: an open notebook with a fountain pen at the edge OR a matte ceramic mug of tea at the corner. Pick ONE. Keep it soft-focus.
- About 20–25% of the frame is empty desk surface so the image breathes when cropped.

- The browser/Notion frame should have a thin clean top bar — a simple title reading "Resonance OS" or "Resonance OS · Founder Workspace" in a sans-serif, small.

DESIGN OF THE NOTION-LIKE UI:
- The interface uses a near-white background with charcoal text. Sans-serif body text. Section headings in a heavier weight. Subtle dividers between blocks.
- Accent details (callout bar, table heading underline) in warm gold #B8973A — NOT Notion's default blue.
- Clean, premium-but-recognizable. The viewer should think "this is Notion, but well-designed."
- No clutter. No notifications. No comments panel open. No second tab.

LIGHTING:
- Soft natural daylight from a tall window OUT OF FRAME (left or right). Warm directional light catches the laptop's hinge and the wood grain.
- Subtle reflections on the screen are acceptable but the UI inside the screen must remain clearly legible — no heavy glare, no wash-out.
- Color temperature warm, around 4500K shifted warm.

TEXTURE / RENDERING:
- Editorial product photography. Full-frame camera, 50mm prime lens feel. Shallow but not extreme depth of field — the laptop screen and table are sharp, the desk edges fall slightly soft.
- Subtle natural film grain (light Kodak Portra 400 emulation).
- No HDR, no over-sharpening.
- The laptop screen content must be rendered crisply — like a real screen captured in a real photograph, not an obvious paste-in.

COLOR PALETTE (must match the brand):
- Desk: warm honey/walnut wood, harmonizing with #F5F2EC and #FAF7F0
- Screen UI: near-white background, charcoal #0D0D0D text, warm gold #B8973A accents
- Light: soft amber-gold, in the family of #B8973A but desaturated
- Shadows: deep warm charcoal, never blue

MOOD: a thoughtful operator's workspace in the morning — the laptop has been just opened, the day hasn't fully started, the work begins quietly. Considered, refined, slow. The Notion workspace itself looks like it was designed by someone who cares about typography.

REFERENCE STYLES:
- Linear.app or Notion's own product photography (laptop on desk with warm light)

- Kinfolk-magazine product features

- NO Lorem ipsum or placeholder gibberish — every visible text must be plausibly real
- NO blue accents — replace any Notion-default blue with the brand gold #B8973A

- NO calendar widgets, chat windows, or unrelated apps
- NO people, hands, fingers reaching for the keyboard
- NO desk clutter — minimal, considered surface
- NO motivational posters on the wall behind, no quote prints, no plants in dramatic vases
- NO modern open-plan office aesthetic, no coworking space
- NO HDR, no over-saturation, no teal-and-orange grade
- NO cool blue light or fluorescent overhead
- NO screen-protector glare or rainbow reflections
- NO obvious AI-rendered text artifacts — if the model can't render the table cleanly, prefer to show the structure with fewer columns rather than garbled text
