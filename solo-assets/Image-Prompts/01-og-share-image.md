A social-share card for a premium founder-coaching landing page. Aspect ratio ~1.9:1, designed to be cropped to 1200×630 for Open Graph. Editorial, calm, restrained — like a page from The Economist or Monocle, not a Silicon Valley ad.

COMPOSITION:
- Solid warm aged-paper background, hex #F5F2EC, with extremely subtle paper grain (barely visible — like a high-quality cream uncoated stock photographed in soft light). Negative space dominates the frame.
- Top-center, a small uppercase label in spaced-out gold (#B8973A) sans-serif (letter-spacing wide, around 14pt visual weight): "LIGHT CREATORS". Above this label a thin 1pt horizontal gold rule about 60px wide, centered.
- Center of the canvas: the single word "SOLO" set in an elegant high-contrast serif (Playfair Display or Didot family), regular weight (not bold), dark charcoal #0D0D0D, very large — approximately 30% of the canvas height. Tight letter-spacing. The "O" letters should be perfectly round, classical proportions.
- Directly below "SOLO", a single line of italic serif in muted gold (#B8973A), much smaller (around 5% of canvas height): "Resonance OS + 2h Coach Access". Italic, regular weight.
- To the right of the SOLO wordmark (or floating in the lower-right quadrant), a small geometric line-drawing mark in gold (#B8973A): single-weight hairline strokes, sacred-geometry style — a circle within a triangle within a hexagon, or similar minimal geometric composition. No fill, only strokes. About 8% of canvas width. This mark echoes the icon style of the brand.
- Generous whitespace on all sides. The composition breathes.

LIGHTING / TEXTURE:
- The paper background feels physical — like printed on a premium uncoated stock and photographed under soft diffused daylight. Slight warmth, no shine.
- Type sits flat on the paper, no drop shadows, no glow, no embossing.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC (warm off-white background)
- #0D0D0D (charcoal — main wordmark)
- #B8973A (gold — label, italic line, geometric mark)
- #6B6560 (muted text, used sparingly if needed)

MOOD: calm authority, precise, unhurried. A well-prepared proposal from someone you already respect. Editorial gravitas. Premium-but-restrained.

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No people, no faces, no hands
- No UI screenshots, no app mockups, no Notion-style boxes
- No gradients, no glow, no neon, no lens flares
- No mandalas, no glowing orbs, no AI-cyber aesthetics
- No motivational coaching clichés (mountains, lightbulbs, peaks, ladders, locks, keys)
- No stock-photo handshakes
- No drop shadows on the type
- No exclamation marks, no hype words
- No Google-style Material design
- No fake "as seen in" logos
