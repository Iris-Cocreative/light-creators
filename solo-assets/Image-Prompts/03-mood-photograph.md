A still-life editorial photograph of a wooden desk in warm interior light. Used once on a premium founder-coaching landing page — placed near the "About David" or "Who This Is For" section to set a contemplative, post-conversation mood. The image should feel like a quiet moment captured in a Kinfolk or Cereal magazine essay, not advertising photography.

COMPOSITION:
- Landscape, 3:2 aspect ratio. Shot from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle looking down onto the desk surface.
- A warm honey-toned wooden desk fills most of the frame. The wood grain is visible but quiet — old oak or walnut with a soft matte finish, not glossy.
- On the desk, arranged with intention but not symmetry:
  - An open hardcover notebook with cream pages, slightly off-center. A single fountain pen lies diagonally across the right page. A few handwritten lines are visible but unreadable.
  - A small ceramic mug, matte off-white or natural unglazed, holding tea (warm amber liquid). Faint steam optional — subtle, photographic, not stylized.
  - A small stack of two or three cloth-bound books to one side. Linen-textured spines in muted tones (warm grey, dusty olive, deep navy). Titles unreadable or absent.
  - A small sprig of dried eucalyptus or olive branch, OR a smooth pale stone the size of a small egg.
  - Optional: a single brass paperclip, a folded letter, or a wax-sealed envelope edge — pick at most ONE additional element. Keep the surface uncluttered.
- A tall window is visible at the edge of the frame (left or right edge, partially out of view), or the light suggests one off-frame. Soft daylight pours diagonally across the desk.
- Generous negative space — at least 30% of the frame is empty desk surface or soft shadow.

LIGHTING:
- Single source of soft natural daylight from a tall window. Golden hour quality but indoor — warm, diffused, slightly directional.
- Soft shadows fall to one side, not harsh. The light catches the rim of the mug and the edge of the notebook page.
- Slight gold-warm color temperature throughout, around 4500K shifted warm.
- Optional very faint dust particles caught in the light beam — barely visible.

TEXTURE / RENDERING:
- Photorealistic editorial photography. Shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm or 85mm prime lens. Shallow depth of field — the notebook and mug are sharp, the books and background fall slightly off-focus.
- Subtle natural film grain — like a high-quality digital file with a touch of Kodak Portra 400 emulation. Not stylized, not over-graded.
- No post-production color shifts or filters. The image looks like it was taken on a quiet morning, not retouched for a campaign.

COLOR PALETTE (must match the brand):
- Background and desk: warm honey wood tones harmonizing with #F5F2EC (warm off-white) and #FAF7F0 (paper-cream)
- Light: soft amber-gold, in the family of #B8973A (gold accent)
- Shadows: deep charcoal, #0D0D0D, never blue
- Mid-tones: muted, in the family of #6B6560
- No other color casts — no cool blues, no greens beyond muted dried foliage

MOOD: contemplative, post-conversation, "the moment after a deep call when you're about to commit". Unhurried. Refined without being precious. The kind of desk a thoughtful operator works at — not a startup founder's gaming setup.

REFERENCE STYLES (in order of priority):
- Kinfolk magazine still-life photography (slow living, considered objects)
- Cereal magazine quiet interiors
- The Gentleman's Journal editorial product features
- Cig Harvey's color and light work
- Old-world stationery brand campaigns (Smythson, Mark + Graham)

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No people, no hands reaching into frame, no body parts
- No laptops, no phones, no cables, no chargers, no monitors, no keyboards
- No corporate office aesthetics — this is not an open-plan desk
- No motivational coaching props (vision boards, sticky notes with goals, gratitude lists)
- No quotes painted on walls, no framed prints with text
- No coffee chains, no branded cups, no logos visible on any object
- No cluttered surfaces — fewer objects, more space
- No flowers in vases (a dried sprig laid flat is fine; a styled bouquet is not)
- No HDR, no over-sharpened detail, no high-contrast Instagram look
- No teal-and-orange color grade, no cool-toned shadows
- No glowing crystals, no sage smoke, no incense (this is editorial, not wellness)
- No exclamation-mark energy — the entire image should feel like an exhale
