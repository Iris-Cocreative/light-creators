A wide atmospheric panel for use as an ambient horizontal band on a premium founder-coaching landing page — placed between sections as a visual breath. This is a sister to 06-window-light-panel.png (morning light) and 09-atmospheric-panel-morning.png (late morning light). This image takes the SAME world to a DIFFERENT TIME OF DAY: late afternoon, warmer, lower-angled, slightly more golden.

CRITICAL: This is a MOOD panel, not a hero image. Almost-empty. Text or a button must be able to sit on top of it without struggle.

COMPOSITION:
- Wide landscape, ~21:9 aspect ratio.
- The frame is a warm cream natural-linen surface (subtle weave visible but quiet) — or a fine cream paper if linen reads too rustic. Same family of warm off-white as the rest of the brand, slightly warmer/honeyed than image 06.
- A LOW-ANGLE shaft of late-afternoon light rakes across the surface horizontally — almost parallel to the frame's bottom edge, picking up the weave of the linen and casting soft long shadows from the texture itself. The light enters from the RIGHT side of the frame (different again from images 06 and 09, so the three panels feel distinct).
- A single small detail object lives near the CENTER-LEFT — far from the previous panels' compositions:
  - A smooth pale river stone the size of a small egg, casting a soft long shadow because of the low light. Just the one stone.
  - OR: a single thin gold-thread bookmark resting in a slight curl, casting a hairline shadow.
  - Pick ONE. Soft-focus, never sharp.
- About 70% of the frame is empty surface so text can be placed across the middle horizontal band.

LIGHTING:
- Low-angle late afternoon golden light from the right, raking nearly horizontally. Slightly warmer than morning light — more honeyed.
- The light makes the surface texture visible — subtle weave or fine paper grain catches the light, but never dramatically.
- Color temperature warmer than the morning panels, around 4200K. Still natural, never artificial.
- Long soft shadow from the detail object, falling to the left.

TEXTURE / RENDERING:
- Linen weave OR fine paper grain, visible but never dominant.
- Subtle natural film grain (light Kodak Portra 400 emulation).
- Editorial photography quality.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC — base surface tone
- #FBF8F2 — brightest lit ridge of the weave
- #F0EBE0 to #E8E0CF — softly shadowed area (slightly warmer than morning panels)
- Light has a warm gold cast in the family of #B8973A, slightly more saturated than the morning panels but still natural
- Any incidental detail (stone, bookmark, paperclip) in muted naturals — soft pale grey-cream, brushed brass

REFERENCE STYLES:
- A late-afternoon Kinfolk still-life
- Cig Harvey's golden-hour interior work
- A Vermeer-adjacent quality of low light on cloth (but pulled toward editorial photography, not painterly)

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No people, no body parts
- No furniture filling the frame
- No bright hot spots — the brightest pixel still feels like sunlit cream, not light source
- No motivational coaching imagery
- No god rays from above — light is LOW and from the side
- No teal-and-orange grade, no cool tones
- No HDR, no over-sharpening
- No text, no watermarks, no logos
- NO eucalyptus sprig (those belong to other panels in this set)
- NO folded letter or paperclip (those belong to panel 09)
- NO direct repetition of the compositions in panels 06 or 09 — the three together must feel like a sequence, not duplicates
