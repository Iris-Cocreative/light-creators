A single-line ink illustration of a relaxed human right hand on warm off-white paper. This is the centerpiece for the "Resonance Hand" concept on a premium founder-coaching landing page. The hand must feel HUMAN — drawn by a person, not engineered. Organic, breathing, slightly imperfect. NOT an anatomical textbook diagram. NOT a robot or wireframe.

CRITICAL: the previous attempt looked robotic — flat fingertips, segmented joints like a marionette, fingers like dowels. AVOID all of that. The new hand should look like it was drawn quickly and confidently with a fountain pen in a notebook — a single gesture, alive on the page.

COMPOSITION:
- Portrait 4:5 orientation. Hand occupies about 55% of the frame, centered with generous whitespace on all four sides.
- View: the back of a relaxed right hand seen from slightly above, fingers naturally spread but not stiff. The hand should feel like it just came to rest — there is a slight curl in the fingers, NOT extended straight. Natural anatomy: thumb shorter and set off to one side, middle finger longest, pinky shortest. Soft, rounded knuckles. Tapered fingertips that end in soft points (not blunt cylinders).
- Drawing style: continuous flowing ink line in gold (#B8973A) on warm off-white (#F5F2EC) paper. Single-weight or very gently varied line — like a fountain pen with a flexible nib. Slight wobble and human imperfection in the line is welcome; over-precision is not.
- NO interior joint lines, NO knuckle creases, NO segmented finger markings, NO fingernails, NO wrinkles. Just the outline of the hand and the very subtle suggestion of palm/finger separation where one finger meets another (a brief inner line where needed for clarity, nothing more).
- Five short labels in handwritten or hand-set italic serif (Playfair Display Italic feel) in gold (#B8973A), placed near each fingertip with a SHORT thin gold line (1pt) connecting the label to the corresponding finger. Labels lowercase or sentence-case, NOT uppercase:
  - Thumb: "Obsession"
  - Index finger: "Presence"
  - Middle finger: "Vision"
  - Ring finger: "Founder Fit"
  - Pinky / little finger: "Scalable Logic"
- Labels feel like they were annotated onto the drawing — slightly off-axis, naturally placed, not perfectly aligned in a grid.

REFERENCE STYLES (in order of priority — ANCHOR THE MODEL HERE):
- Henri Matisse single-line figure drawings (one continuous fluid line, organic curves)
- Egon Schiele's quick ink hand studies (gestural, alive, expressive contours)
- Pablo Picasso line drawings of hands and figures (minimalism, confident curves)
- Saul Steinberg New Yorker line work (witty, considered, hand-feel)
- A page from a thoughtful person's leather notebook — sketched while thinking, not rendered as a final diagram

LINE QUALITY:
- The line varies slightly — slightly thicker on the down-strokes, thinner where the pen lifts, like a real fountain pen. Not perfectly uniform.
- There may be one or two tiny breaks in the line where the hand lifted, suggesting it was drawn in two or three confident motions, not generated.
- The line is confident, not scratchy or sketchy. No hatching. No shading. No fill.

PAGE / BACKGROUND:
- Warm off-white #F5F2EC paper with extremely subtle paper grain — like a cream uncoated stock photographed in soft daylight. No texture overlay heavier than that.
- No frame, no border, no decoration. The drawing floats in space.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC — paper background
- #B8973A — every line, every label

MOOD: calm, considered, human. The hand of someone who has thought about this. Editorial, not mystical. Premium-but-quiet.

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- NO robotic, mechanical, prosthetic, or wireframe aesthetics
- NO segmented joint markings on the fingers (the previous version had small horizontal ticks across each finger — DO NOT include these)
- NO blunt cylindrical fingertips — fingertips taper softly
- NO flat 2D vector look — the line should breathe
- NO interior anatomy lines, no joint creases, no fingernails
- NO mandalas, sacred-geometry overlays, flower-of-life patterns, or grid lines inside the hand
- NO mystical glow, halos, light rays, or aura
- NO 3D rendering, photorealism, or shading
- NO color other than the gold and the cream paper
- NO uppercase labels (the existing labels were UPPERCASE and felt clinical — use sentence case in italic serif instead)
- NO heavy ornamental tick marks connecting labels — use a single thin hairline
- NO symmetrical perfect-pose hand — the hand should feel relaxed, slightly asymmetric, alive
- NO Da Vinci anatomy-textbook diagram aesthetic (the previous prompt over-anchored here — pull AWAY from this)
