A wide atmospheric panel for use as an ambient horizontal band on a premium founder-coaching landing page — placed between sections as a visual breath. This is a sister image to two existing panels on the page: 06-window-light-panel.png (soft window light with eucalyptus sprig and notebook corner) and 03-mood-photograph.png (warm wooden desk still-life). The three should form a quiet family — same light, same world, different moments — without ever feeling repetitive.

CRITICAL: This is a MOOD panel, not a hero image. Almost-empty. Text or a button must be able to sit on top of it without struggle. The image should feel like a held breath.

COMPOSITION:
- Wide landscape, ~21:9 aspect ratio. Designed to live as a horizontal band between sections.
- The frame is mostly a warm cream paper or soft fabric surface (#F5F2EC). Compared to image 06 (which had light from the upper-right with a sprig at lower-left), this image MUST be DIFFERENT in composition — the light direction reverses, and the detail object changes — so the two panels never read as the same image.
- Soft diagonal shaft of morning light enters from the UPPER-LEFT, falling across the frame toward the lower-right. The brightest area peaks at roughly #FBF8F2; unlit areas sit at #F0EBE0. Very low contrast — entire image in a 6–8% tonal range.
- A small detail object lives in the LOWER-RIGHT corner (NOT lower-left — that's where image 06 placed its sprig):
  - Pick one: the edge of a cream folded letter or notecard with a thin wax seal, OR a small pale stone the size of an egg, OR a single loose page lying flat with the corner curled, OR a brass paperclip resting on a folded piece of paper.
  - Whatever you pick, render it small, soft-focus, and quiet — never centered.
- About 75% of the frame is empty surface so text can be placed across the middle horizontal band.

LIGHTING:
- Soft directional morning light from the upper-left. The light is filtered, diffused, slightly warm — golden hour quality but indoor.
- Optional very faint dust particles caught in the light beam — barely visible.
- No hard edges. No spotlights. No HDR.

TEXTURE / RENDERING:
- Subtle paper grain or soft linen weave throughout (decide whichever reads more refined for this composition — paper preferred).
- Subtle natural film grain, very light. Suggestive of Kodak Portra 400 emulation but barely there.
- Editorial photography quality.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC — base paper tone
- #FBF8F2 — brightest lit area (no brighter)
- #F0EBE0 — softly shadowed area (no darker)
- Light has a warm cast in the family of #B8973A but desaturated — like sunlight, not a colored gel
- Any incidental detail (letter, stone, paper edge, paperclip) in muted naturals — soft cream, brushed brass, warm putty

REFERENCE STYLES:
- The opening photograph of a Kinfolk magazine article
- An empty Cereal magazine page right before the chapter title
- Light hitting a blank page in a Cig Harvey color study

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No people, no body parts
- No furniture filling the frame — this is a quiet surface, not a styled scene
- No bright hot spots
- No motivational coaching imagery (sunbeams through forest, mountain dawn)
- No god rays from above — light is from the upper-left, low and quiet
- No vignette, no dark corners
- No teal-and-orange grade, no cool tones, no greens beyond very dusty olive
- No HDR, no over-sharpening
- No text, no watermarks, no logos
- NO eucalyptus sprig at lower-left (06 already uses that — this image needs a different detail in a different corner)
- NO repetition of image 06's exact composition
