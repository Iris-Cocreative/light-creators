A wide atmospheric panel for use as an ambient horizontal band on a premium founder-coaching landing page — possibly between major sections or behind a single CTA. The panel is almost-but-not-quite empty: it sets a mood without competing for attention.

CRITICAL: This is a MOOD panel, not a hero image. It must work as a background — text or a button can sit on top of it without struggle. The image should feel like a quiet morning, a held breath.

COMPOSITION:
- Wide landscape, ~21:9 aspect ratio. Designed to live as a horizontal band.
- The frame is mostly warm off-white paper (#F5F2EC) with a soft diagonal shaft of warmer window light crossing from one corner toward the opposite. Imagine looking at a blank cream notebook page on a desk just as morning sun reaches it.
- The light shaft is gentle, diffused, soft-edged — NOT a hard sun beam, NOT a stage spotlight. The brightest area peaks at roughly #FBF8F2 (very subtle warm white) and the unlit areas sit at #F0EBE0 (paper slightly in shadow). Very low contrast overall — the whole image is in a 6–8% tonal range.
- Optional: one tiny detail in the lower third — the corner of a notebook page or a single eucalyptus leaf casting a soft shadow, sitting just at the edge. Keep it almost-invisible, an afterthought, never centered.
- No people, no objects in the main visual area. Generous negative space across the entire middle horizontal band so text can be placed there.

LIGHTING:
- Soft directional morning light from one side (left or right — either works; consistency within the frame matters more than which side). The light is filtered, diffused, slightly warm — golden hour quality but indoor.
- Subtle dust particles in the light beam are welcome if barely visible.
- No hard edges. No spotlights. No HDR.

TEXTURE:
- Slight paper grain throughout, like a high-quality cream uncoated stock photographed under soft daylight.
- Subtle natural film grain, very light. Suggestive of Kodak Portra 400 emulation but barely there.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC — base paper tone
- #FBF8F2 — brightest lit area (no brighter)
- #F0EBE0 — softly shadowed area (no darker)
- Light has a warm cast in the family of #B8973A (gold) but desaturated — like sunlight, not a colored gel
- Any incidental detail (notebook corner, leaf) renders in muted naturals — warm wood tone, dusty olive, pale stone

REFERENCE STYLES:
- The negative-space photograph in a Kinfolk article opener
- Light hitting a blank page in a Cig Harvey color study
- An empty Cereal magazine page right before the chapter title
- A still photograph of morning light from a James Turrell-adjacent sensibility (but on paper, not in an architectural void)

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No people, no body parts
- No furniture filling the frame — this is a quiet surface, not a styled scene
- No bright highlights or hot spots — the brightest pixel should still feel like paper, not light source
- No strong shadows or chiaroscuro
- No motivational coaching imagery (sunbeams through forest, mountain dawn, etc.)
- No god rays from above — the light is from the side, low and quiet
- No vignette, no dark corners
- No gradients that read as "synthetic" — every light transition is in the family of natural paper
- No teal-and-orange grade, no cool tones, no greens beyond very dusty olive
- No HDR, no over-sharpening, no Instagram filter look
- No text, no watermarks, no logos
