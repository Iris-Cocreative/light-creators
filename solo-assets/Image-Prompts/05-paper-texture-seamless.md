A subtle seamless tileable paper texture for use as a CSS background-image on a premium founder-coaching landing page. Designed to be repeated across the page and overlaid on the existing #F5F2EC base color at low opacity (~20–35%) to add warmth and physicality without ever calling attention to itself.

CRITICAL: This is NOT a decorative texture. It is a near-invisible surface treatment. If a viewer notices the texture as "noise" or "grain" at first glance, the prompt has failed. The goal is the quiet feeling of looking at premium uncoated paper instead of a flat screen color.

COMPOSITION:
- Square 1024×1024, designed as a SEAMLESS tile (the left edge must align with the right edge, and the top edge with the bottom — no visible seams when repeated).
- Uniform overall — no focal point, no center of interest, no directional light, no vignette, no gradient, no patches of variation that would create obvious repetition when tiled.
- Microscopic fiber texture: very faint, like the long irregular fibers in handmade cotton paper or a premium uncoated text stock (think Mohawk Superfine, Crane Lettra, or Arjowiggins Curious Skin).
- Tiny natural specks: 4–8 almost-invisible darker flecks per square inch of equivalent area, irregularly distributed (like real paper inclusions). Specks should be at most 2–3 pixels and very low contrast.
- Fine random grain: a subtle photographic-feeling tooth, like the rough surface of cream uncoated paper photographed at 1× magnification under soft diffused daylight.

TONE / COLOR:
- Base color #F5F2EC (warm off-white). The texture variation should sit within roughly 4–6% lightness of this base — never going darker than #EDE7D8 or lighter than #FBF8F2.
- Warm cream temperature throughout. NO cool blue or grey tones. NO yellowing toward beige (the existing texture-paper.png in this brand's library is too yellow — pull AWAY from that direction).
- No areas darker than #EDE7D8. No areas brighter than #FBF8F2. The entire image is a very narrow tonal range.

LIGHTING:
- Flat. No directional light. No highlights. No shadows. This is a seamless tile, not a photograph of paper in a scene.

REFERENCE STYLES:
- A scanned blank page of a Smythson notebook
- Crane Lettra 110# Pearl White stock under flatbed scanner conditions
- A patch of unprinted area on a high-end magazine spread (Cereal, Kinfolk, Apartamento)

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No directional light, no vignette, no gradient, no central glow
- No visible repetition pattern or obvious tiles
- No heavy fibers, no canvas weave, no linen texture (this is paper, not fabric)
- No coffee stains, no creases, no folds, no torn edges
- No yellowed antique parchment look — this is fresh premium paper, just warm-cream
- No watermark, no embossed crest, no printed marks
- No noise that reads as "digital grain" — the texture should feel physical, not pixelated
- No colors outside the #F5F2EC ±4% lightness range
- No focal point of any kind — the tile must be uniform under inspection
