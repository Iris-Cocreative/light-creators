An editorial line-drawing of a human right hand, viewed palm-out, fingers extended and slightly fanned. Used as the centerpiece illustration for the "Resonance Hand" concept on a premium founder-coaching landing page. The hand is a teaching diagram, not a portrait — calm, instructional, contemplative.

COMPOSITION:
- Portrait orientation, 4:5 aspect. Hand centered, occupying about 60% of the frame. Generous whitespace on all four sides.
- The hand is drawn in pure single-weight gold (#B8973A) line on warm off-white (#F5F2EC) background. Hairline strokes — uniform thickness throughout, like a technical or anatomical illustration.
- Style: matches a family of existing sacred-geometry icons — single-stroke, geometric, no fill, no shading, no skin texture, no cross-hatching. Imagine a hand drawn by combining clean anatomical line work with sacred-geometry diagram precision.
- Each finger is straight and slightly separated from the others. The fingers should feel deliberate, almost diagrammatic — like a Da Vinci proportions sketch rendered with the restraint of an Edward Tufte information-design illustration.
- At each fingertip, a small uppercase sans-serif label in gold (#B8973A) at approximately 10pt visual weight, letter-spacing slightly expanded:
  - Thumb: "OBSESSION"
  - Index finger: "PRESENCE"
  - Middle finger: "VISION"
  - Ring finger: "FOUNDER FIT"
  - Pinky / little finger: "SCALABLE LOGIC"
- Each label sits above the corresponding fingertip with a small connecting tick mark or thin line (1pt gold) leading from the fingertip to the label — like an anatomical diagram callout.
- Background is solid #F5F2EC paper, very subtle paper grain only — no texture noise, no patterns.

LIGHTING / RENDERING:
- Flat 2D line drawing. No 3D shading, no light direction. Pure illustration, no photographic quality.
- The line is even, confident, drafted as if by a steady hand using a fine technical pen.

COLOR PALETTE (exact, no others):
- #F5F2EC (warm off-white background)
- #B8973A (gold — every line and label)
- Optional: #6B6560 for labels only if gold reads too light against the paper

MOOD: calm, instructional, editorial. A premium textbook diagram. Sacred-geometry restraint without mysticism. The hand should feel like it could appear in a Kinfolk magazine alongside an essay about embodied leadership.

REFERENCE STYLES (in order of priority):
- The icon set this image accompanies: single-stroke gold sacred-geometry symbols on warm paper
- Tom Froese editorial line work
- Diagram pages in "The Visual Display of Quantitative Information" by Edward Tufte
- Anatomical hand drawings from a 19th-century medical text — but pared back to pure geometric line

AVOID ABSOLUTELY:
- No realism, no skin texture, no fingernails, no wrinkles, no veins
- No shadows, no shading, no gradients, no fills
- No color other than the gold and the paper background
- No mystical aura, no glowing energy, no chakras, no aura halos, no light rays
- No mandalas, no flower-of-life patterns (the lines are the structure, not added ornament)
- No watercolor look, no painterly textures
- No 3D rendering, no photorealism
- No additional symbols inside the palm — keep the palm space mostly empty so labels read clearly
- No coaching clichés (no rising sun, no compass, no peaks)
- The fingers should NOT curl inward or form gestures — they are extended for the diagram
