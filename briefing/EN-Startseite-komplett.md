## How I Work

**Eyebrow**
HOW I WORK

**H2**
Future intelligence emerges in generative dialogue.

**Absatz 1**
Many people in my field still believe their clients need more information. In
my experience, the real problem is not a lack of information.

**Absatz 2**
What is needed is space to recognise what is essential for each of us, to hear
the whisper of the future, and connection to turn that recognition into
concrete results. For this I have developed a method with four steps. I work
with it in executive coaching online, in workshops with leadership teams, and
in the forests of Finland. The process stays the same. What changes is the
place.

**01 Letting go. Making space.**
*So that what is old and non-essential can go and space can open again.*
In the organisation: letting go of old certainties and old knowledge. Saying
out loud what no longer carries, even if it was right for years.
In the forest: making space to hear the whisper of the future. Two days
without a feed and without everyone else's opinion.

**02 Connecting. Back into contact.**
*Because development succeeds better in relationship than alone.*
In the organisation: safe spaces where difficult emotions also have room and
can be worked through. Only then can the threshold to the new be crossed.
In the forest: recognising yourself by being witnessed and reflected by
others. Development happens in relationship.

**03 Recognising. Developing a positive vision of the future.**
*So that inspiration and future intelligence can emerge.*
In the organisation: recognising and creating well-founded confidence.
Developing new strategies together, releasing collective strengths, and
building a future from them that will hold.
In the forest: recognising your own strengths and your own contribution,
without measuring them against a feed.

**04 Shaping. Building the next step.**
*So that recognition becomes concrete next steps.*
In the organisation: a decision the team carries, and a development sprint
that ends with a first concrete result.
Back from the forest: one concrete next step and a buddy system at your side,
with which you achieve something you would not have achieved alone.
