# 2 · /threshold/partner/en/

**Title:** Fund a place · The Threshold Program
**Description:** For parents, foundations and companies who want to make a
place in The Threshold Program possible for a young adult.

## Hero

**Eyebrow**
THE THRESHOLD PROGRAM

**H1**
Make a place possible.

**Text**
Threshold is a six-day programme for young adults between 18 and 28 who face a
decision that will shape what follows. Two guided days alone in nature, a small
group, one concrete next step and six weeks of support afterwards.

## Helping without prescribing

**H2**
Helping, without prescribing the way.

**Absatz 1**
Parents know this tension. You see that your child is at a point where
something is being decided. You have experience, opinions and usually a fairly
clear sense of what would be good. And at the same time you know: the more
clearly you say it, the less it becomes their own answer.

**Absatz 2**
Threshold does not take this relationship from you and does not replace
parental guidance. For six days it creates the conditions in which your child
can find out for themselves what their next step is. Without your opinion in
the room. And without anyone else's.

## Why these years

**H2**
Why these years. And why now.

**Absatz 1**
Between 18 and 28, much of what a life later draws on is decided. Which
direction someone takes. By what standard they measure it. Whether they stay
capable of acting under pressure. It is also the phase of life with the least
support. School is over, an employer is not yet responsible, and parents are
not neutral.

**Absatz 2**
There is a second shift, and it concerns every organisation. The more routine
is automated, the more depends on what a person can contribute that is not a
copy of something that already exists. That question cannot be read up on. It
needs a place where no one prescribes the outcome.

**Drei Zellen**

Self-knowledge
Someone who has worked out their own strengths still knows them in five years.
A test result is known until the next folder.

Innovation from clarity
People who have found their own ground move differently under pressure. They
build instead of freezing.

Agency in uncertainty
Taking a real step without waiting for the next instruction. That is precisely
what a machine cannot do.

## Who funds the places

**H2**
Who funds the places

**Text**
Some participants pay themselves. Some are supported by their parents. And some
places are covered by foundations, educational institutions or employers who
want to open access for young people who could not otherwise afford it.

## What you receive

**H2**
What you receive

**Text**
For scholarships: a written reflection from the person you supported, with
their consent. Not marketing material, but the words of a young person about
what has changed.

## Ways of working together

**H2**
Ways of working together

**Scholarship Partner**
Fund one or more places for young adults who could not otherwise take part.
Your support is credited directly to the people you help, and with their
consent you receive a short written reflection on what has changed.

**Impact Partner**
Receive access to anonymised before-and-after reflections and outcome notes
from each cohort. Built for foundations and institutions that track what
actually builds resilience in young people.

**Employer and Talent Partner**
Send your own emerging talent or leaders in transition. The place is positioned
internally as an investment in their development.

**Founding Partner**
This is the first Threshold group. Those who carry the programme through this
first year are named Founding Partners of The Threshold Program, with
visibility in future materials and first access to the next groups.

## Costs

**H2**
Costs

**Absatz 1**
An introductory rate applies for the founding group. We discuss the cost per
place in conversation.

**Absatz 2**
In 30 minutes we explore together whether this programme is the right one, how
the six days unfold and what value it creates.
