The Threshold Program

Make a place possible.

Threshold is a six-day programme for young adults between 18 and 28 who face a decision that will shape what follows. Two guided days alone in nature, a small group, one concrete next step and six weeks of support afterwards.

Helping, without prescribing the way.

Parents know this tension. You see that your child is at a point where something is being decided. You have experience, opinions and usually a fairly clear sense of what would be good. And at the same time you know: the more clearly you say it, the less it becomes their own answer.

Threshold does not take this relationship from you and does not replace parental guidance. For six days it creates the conditions in which your child can find out for themselves what their next step is. Without your opinion in the room. And without anyone else’s.

Funding

Who funds the places.

Some participants pay themselves. Some are supported by their parents. And some places are covered by foundations, educational institutions or employers who want to open access for young people who could not otherwise afford it.

In return

What you receive.

For scholarships: a written reflection from the person you supported, with their consent. Not marketing material, but the words of a young person about what has changed.

Partnership with Threshold

Why these years. And why now.

Between 18 and 28, much of what a life later draws on is decided. Which direction someone takes. By what standard they measure it. Whether they stay capable of acting under pressure. It is also the phase of life with the least support. School is over, an employer is not yet responsible, and parents are not neutral.

There is a second shift, and it concerns every organisation. The more routine is automated, the more depends on what a person can contribute that is not a copy of something that already exists. That question cannot be read up on. It needs a place where no one prescribes the outcome.

Self-knowledge
Someone who has worked out their own strengths still knows them in five years. A test result is known until the next folder.
Innovation from clarity
People who have found their own ground move differently under pressure. They build instead of freezing.
Agency in uncertainty
Taking a real step without waiting for the next instruction. That is precisely what a machine cannot do.

Funding a place in this founding group is an investment in exactly that resilience, at the point in a young person’s life where six days can shape decisions that last for years.

Ways of working together
Scholarship Partner
Fund one or more places for young adults who could not otherwise take part. Your support is credited directly to the people you help, and with their consent you receive a short written reflection on what has changed.
Impact Partner
Receive access to anonymised before-and-after reflections and outcome notes from each cohort. Built for foundations and institutions that track what actually builds resilience in young people.
Employer and Talent Partner
Send your own emerging talent or leaders in transition. The place is positioned internally as an investment in their development.
Founding Partner
This is the first Threshold group. Those who carry the programme through this first year are named Founding Partners of The Threshold Program, with visibility in future materials and first access to the next groups.
