Headline:  
Frequently asked questions

Q1: Is this really free?  
Yes. 60 minutes, free.  
You get clarity about your gap and concrete steps – regardless of whether we work together afterward.

Q2: Why do I need to apply first?  
I only accept founders where I can truly make a difference. The application helps me understand if we're a fit. It takes 5-10 minutes and saves both of us time.

Q3: How long until I hear back?  
Within 48 hours. Usually faster.

Q4: What if my application is declined?  
I'll send you honest feedback on why the timing isn't right yet – and what to work on first. Sometimes the answer is: "Come back in 3-6 months."

Q5: What happens at the end of the call?  
You get honest feedback about your situation – even if the answer is: "Not ready for High-Touch yet." That happens. More often than you think.

At the end, I tell you what I recommend: Solo Coaching, THRESHOLD, Notion OS, or "work on your own for another 3 months."

No pressure. Just clarity.

Q6: What happens after the call?  
You receive an email with:

→ Summary of your gap  
→ 1-3 Micro-Shifts (immediately actionable)  
→ My recommendation (SOLO / THRESHOLD / Notion OS / Wait)

Then you decide whether and how to continue.

Q7: Can I cancel or reschedule?  
Yes. You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the call.
