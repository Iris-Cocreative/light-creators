The truth behind the everyday

You're juggling three roles simultaneously.

Entrepreneur

You develop strategy, secure funding, navigate stakeholder relationships.

Manager

You lead your team, build processes, scale systems.

Expert

You work with customers, deliver expertise, solve problems.

Three roles. Simultaneously. And nobody taught you how to switch between them — without burning out.

Here's the truth: successful relationships are your greatest lever. Whether you align your team. Whether you convince investors. Whether you navigate co-founder conflicts. Whether you evaluate exit options with PE funds.

The competence is always the same: how you design strategically critical relationships so they work for you — not against you.

And all relationships begin with one: the one with yourself.

The gap in the market

What's missing.

Pitch coaches optimise slides. Accelerators optimise KPIs. Leadership coaches talk about authenticity.

All correct. All incomplete.

What's missing is systematic access to Leadership Intelligence at all levels. Not performance. Not mindset. Not wellness.

But:

  • Presence under pressure
  • Strategic relationship leadership
  • Inner clarity amid outer complexity
How I work

Leadership begins within — works outward.

I work with entrepreneurs on the one variable that influences trust, funding, and team alignment: your inner operating system.

When your inner system is clear, your pitch becomes calmer, your communication more precise, your team more stable — and investors become more committed. I call this Resonance.

Obsession

Sustainable drive, not toxic overexertion.

Presence

Congruence between body, words, and energy.

Vision Resonance

A vision others can feel.

Founder Fit

Your authentic connection to this mission.

Scalable Logic

The right timing for the right solution.

When even one of these factors is weak, investors sense it — without being able to name it.

They don't say: „Your obsession feels toxic.“ They say: „Interesting — but not for us right now.“

This is the Resonance Gap. And this is exactly where I come in.

About David

Why I do this work.

I've worked with people in positions of responsibility for over 25 years. Trainer. Coach. Facilitator.

In international leadership consultancies, I developed programmes — and as Client Director and Member of the Executive Board, I personally acquired and managed million-dollar projects. Led international teams. Built strategic client relationships.

Accompanied over 20,000 leaders and entrepreneurs in 25 countries — from team leads to C-suite.

I know leadership from operational reality. And I know its price.

In my early 40s, I stood on the edge of inner collapse. Not because I was incapable. But because I had been functioning for too long.

In my early 50s, I made a conscious decision: no longer to stabilise systems that aren't my own. But to work with people who are building their own systems. Entrepreneurs.

The common thread

My common thread has always been the same: clarifying success-critical relationships — vertical and horizontal.

Vertical: The relationship to vision, energy, intuition.
Horizontal: The relationship to team, stakeholders, investors.

Brilliant strategies fail when these relationships don't carry them. I've seen this for two decades. Today, I translate this experience for entrepreneurs who want to lead more consciously.

Vision Quest — where it began

My work began long before my professional life.

At 22, I undertook a traditional vision quest on Vision Mountain in Washington State — four days and nights alone in nature, fasting, asking:

Who am I? What is my contribution?

The answer that emerged has guided me ever since: the healing of relationships — between people, within teams, and between leaders and the deeper intelligence that wants to work through their efforts.

This purpose became the common thread of everything I've done since.
